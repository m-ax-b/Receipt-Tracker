How the loop works

The system does not jump straight to AI. It moves from evidence capture to governed reuse through a deliberate loop.

1

Observe

Approved rows and repair actions become evidence that can be reused later.

2

Reason with OpenClaw

AI is used only when deterministic logic and existing learned patterns are insufficient, then routed into explicit review lanes with agent/model telemetry.

3

Propose

Repeated evidence becomes conservative reusable bundles instead of silent automatic mutation.

4

Approve

Human approval remains the trust boundary before anything becomes active runtime knowledge.

5

Reuse

Approved bundles guide future review, reduce repetitive cleanup, and lower unnecessary AI calls.

Selective AI, not AI everywhere

The system does not send everything to a model. It uses deterministic logic first, then learned patterns, then OpenClaw only for unresolved ambiguity.

Human-governed activation

The system can observe and propose, but it does not silently promote patterns into production behavior.

Reusable knowledge compounds

Approved bundles reduce repetitive cleanup and improve consistency over time.
